Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions about our services, materials, and processes.

Accudent Dental Lab offers a comprehensive range of materials including:

  • IPS e.max® (lithium disilicate)
  • Zirconia (various translucency levels)
  • Porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM)
  • Full-cast metals (precious and non-precious)
  • PMMA provisionals
  • Composite materials

For detailed information about each material, please visit our Materials page.

Our standard turnaround times are as follows:

  • Crown & Bridge: 5-7 working days
  • Implant restorations: 7-10 working days
  • Dentures and removables: 10-14 working days

Rush services are available for an additional fee. Please contact our customer service team for availability.

Submitting a digital case is easy with our online portal:

  1. Log in to your Accudent account (or register if you don't have one)
  2. Click on "Send a Case" in the navigation menu
  3. Fill out the patient and case details
  4. Upload your STL or other supported files
  5. Review your submission and click "Submit Case"

For a detailed walkthrough, please watch our video tutorial on digital case submission.

We accept the following digital impression file formats:

  • STL (Standard Tessellation Language)
  • PLY (Polygon File Format)
  • OBJ (Wavefront Object)

We also accept files from most major intraoral scanner systems, including iTero, CEREC, Trios, Carestream, and more. Files should be uploaded as STL or in a ZIP archive.

You can track the status of your case in several ways:

  • Log in to your account and visit the "My Cases" section to see real-time status updates
  • Check your email for automated notifications about case progress
  • Contact our customer service team with your case number for immediate assistance

Our tracking system provides updates at key milestones: case received, in production, quality check, and shipping.
