Noritake CZR: Exceptional Aesthetics, Unmatched Strength

Noritake CZR represents the pinnacle of zirconia technology, offering an ideal balance of strength and aesthetics for modern dental restorations.

With its unique composition and manufacturing process, Noritake CZR provides excellent translucency while maintaining the strength required for long-term success in the most demanding clinical situations. The material's enhanced light transmission and chameleon effect ensure natural-looking results that blend seamlessly with surrounding dentition.

  • High translucency for natural aesthetics
  • Exceptional strength for all clinical situations
  • Excellent fit and marginal integrity
  • Biocompatible and metal-free

Mechanical Properties
  • Flexural Strength: 900 MPa
  • Fracture Toughness: 6.8 MPa·m¹/²
  • Hardness: 1,250 HV
  • Density: 6.02 g/cm³
  • Young's Modulus: 205 GPa
Optical Properties
  • Translucency: High
  • Light Transmission: 40%
  • Shade: A1-D4
  • Surface Roughness: 0.25-0.45 μm
  • Refractive Index: 2.15
Processing
  • Sintering Temp: 1,500°C
  • Dwell Time: 2 hours
  • Heating Rate: 6°C/min
  • Cooling Rate: 12°C/min
  • Finishing: Diamond polishing
Noritake CZR Shade Guide

Noritake CZR is available in all Vita Classical shades (A1-D4) and can be customized to match any shade requirement. The material's high translucency and excellent light transmission ensure natural-looking results that blend seamlessly with surrounding dentition.
