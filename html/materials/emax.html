e.max: The Gold Standard in Esthetic Dentistry

IPS e.max is the world's leading all-ceramic system, offering unmatched esthetics and strength for anterior and posterior restorations.

With its high strength and natural light transmission, e.max provides the perfect balance of durability and beauty. The lithium disilicate glass-ceramic material is ideal for creating lifelike restorations that blend seamlessly with natural dentition.

Key Benefits:
  • Exceptional esthetics with natural light transmission
  • High flexural strength (400 MPa for Press, 360 MPa for CAD)
  • Minimal tooth preparation required
  • Excellent marginal fit and precision
  • Biocompatible and metal-free

Technical Specifications

Comprehensive technical data for e.max restorations

Mechanical Properties
  • Flexural Strength: 400 MPa (Press), 360 MPa (CAD)
  • Fracture Toughness: 2.25 MPa·m1/2
  • Modulus of Elasticity: 95 GPa
  • Vickers Hardness: 5.8 GPa
  • Density: 2.4 g/cm3
Optical Properties
  • Translucency: High (LT, HT, MO, HO, BL)
  • Refractive Index: 1.55
  • Light Transmission: Up to 60%
  • Fluorescence: Natural tooth-like
  • Opalescence: Natural
Physical Properties
  • CTE: 10.5 × 10-6/K
  • Thermal Conductivity: 1.3 W/(m·K)
  • Chemical Solubility: < 100 μg/cm2
  • Biocompatibility: ISO 10993 & FDA approved
  • Radio-opacity: 2.0 mm Al equivalent
