BruxZir Full Contour: Strength Meets Simplicity

BruxZir Full Contour Zirconia is the original full-contour zirconia restoration, offering exceptional strength and durability for posterior applications.

Designed for patients who require durable, metal-free restorations, BruxZir Full Contour is an excellent choice for posterior crowns and bridges. The material's high flexural strength and fracture resistance make it ideal for patients with bruxism or heavy occlusion.

  • Exceptional strength for posterior restorations
  • Metal-free and biocompatible
  • Excellent wear resistance
  • Precise fit and marginal integrity
