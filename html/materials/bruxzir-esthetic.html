BruxZir Esthetic: Natural Beauty Meets Strength

BruxZir Esthetic Zirconia combines the strength of zirconia with enhanced translucency for restorations that are both beautiful and durable.

With its high translucency and excellent strength, BruxZir Esthetic is ideal for anterior and posterior restorations where aesthetics are a priority. The material's unique composition provides a more natural light transmission similar to natural dentition.

  • Enhanced translucency for natural aesthetics
  • Excellent strength for both anterior and posterior use
  • Biocompatible and metal-free
  • Excellent marginal fit and precision

Mechanical Properties
  • Flexural Strength: 800 MPa
  • Fracture Toughness: 6.5 MPa·m¹/²
  • Hardness: 1,200 HV

Optical Properties
  • Translucency: High
  • Light Transmission: 35%

  • Refractive Index: 2.20
Processing
  • Sintering Temp: 1,450-1,500°C
  • Dwell Time: 2 hours
  • Heating Rate: 5°C/min
  • Cooling Rate: 8°C/min
  • Finishing: Diamond polishing
